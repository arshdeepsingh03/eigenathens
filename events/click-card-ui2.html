EigenAthens

A Free Mathematics Lecture Series

A sequence of informal public lectures developing a mathematical problem from elementary ideas. No specialized mathematical background is assumed. Questions and discussion are encouraged.

Free admission

About the series

Format
Approximately 90 minutes of lecture followed by questions and open discussion.
Frequency
Approximately twice per week.
Meetings
In person.
Audience
Open to the general public. The ideas will be developed progressively from introductory material.
Admission
Free. Registration is used only to estimate attendance and communicate schedule and venue information.
Series span
Beginning in early October 2026 and continuing across a sequence of meetings.

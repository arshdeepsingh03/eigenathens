EigenAthens

A Free Mathematics Lecture Series

A sequence of informal public lectures developing a mathematical problem from the ground up. No specialized mathematical background is assumed. Questions and discussion are welcome after each meeting.

Free admission

About the series

Format
Approximately 90 minutes of lecture followed by questions and open discussion.
Frequency
Approximately twice a week.
Meetings
In person.
Audience
Open to the general public. The material will be developed gradually from elementary ideas.
Admission
Free. Registration is used to estimate attendance and send venue and schedule updates.
Series span
Beginning in early October 2026. The sequence is expected to continue across multiple meetings.
